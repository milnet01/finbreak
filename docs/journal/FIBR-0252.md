# FIBR-0252 — Standard Bank per-row errors reach the import preview

Closed 2026-08-07 (the work and its commits are dated 2026-08-06; the close ran
just past midnight). Contract:
[`docs/specs/FIBR-0252-standard-bank-row-errors.md`](../specs/FIBR-0252-standard-bank-row-errors.md).

## What changed

- **`importers/standard_bank.py`** — one line. `StandardBankImporter.parse`
  ended with `ParseResult(result.drafts, [], …)`; the literal `[]` discarded the
  `RowError`s `_split` had just separated out of the family parse. It now passes
  `result.errors`. No new function, no new field, no signature change — the
  channel and all six of its consumers already existed and were being starved by
  the same empty list on every Standard Bank statement, across all five layout
  families.
- **`tests/features/standard_bank_pdf/fixtures/family_a_zero_fee.pdf`** (new) —
  a synthetic Family A page carrying `SERVICE FEE WAIVED 0.00` between two
  ordinary rows, so it reconciles and previews rather than being refused. 1,840
  bytes; fake payees, the suite's fake account number, the Standard Bank legal
  marker detection needs. Generated with reportlab, which stays probe-only; the
  recipe lives in the spec's § 4.2 because this suite commits no generator.
- **Five new tests** — INV-1/2/3 at the parse boundary; INV-4 in two legs
  (the `error_count` field headless, the rendered Errors cell under `qtbot`);
  INV-5 on the single-file preview; INV-6 guarding the fixture as synthetic.
- **Collateral the fixture caused, both doing exactly their job** — the SB
  suite globs its fixtures directory, so `len(_PRE_E_FIXTURES) == 14` moved to
  `15`, and the hand-written `(filename, expected_family)` detection list gained
  an entry.
- **Both test-suite contracts amended** (the spec's § 12): the SB suite's
  spec.md gained FIBR-0252 in its "Enforces" header, a third `INV-N` numbering,
  the degrade-per-row `RowError` channel it had never documented, the fixture's
  recipe pointer and a coverage-map entry; the batch suite's spec.md had its
  "Synthetic strings only" fixture clause amended and `FIBR-0252 INV-4` listed
  as the one invariant in it that is not FIBR-0085's.

## What was learned

- **The defect was invisible because the balance gate cannot see it.** A dropped
  zero-amount row contributes 0 to every running balance, so `_verify_checksum`
  reconciles happily and `parse` returns normally. The row is gone and — before
  this — nothing anywhere said so. That is why a one-line bug survived this long
  on the path this user's own corpus is made of.
- **A test can be written by cell content and still not test what it claims.**
  The INV-5 leg first asserted a *mapping* keyed on the row-number cell. That is
  order-insensitive, so it could not fail on a re-ordering — while its own
  message said "in file order". Caught by the cold test-quality lane, which
  verified it by mutation (deleting `_fill_preview_table`'s `entries.sort(...)`
  renders `1, 3, 2` and the mapping form stayed green). Now an ordered sequence
  of pairs, re-verified by the same mutation. The spec's `*Test:*` clause had
  prescribed the weaker form, so it was amended in place rather than left
  disagreeing with what was built.
- **The interleave was never actually unguarded** — the pre-existing CSV leg
  `test_import.py::test_INV10c_preview_shows_rows_summary_and_period` goes red on
  the same mutation. § 11 was crediting the wrong leg for it.
- **A `waitUntil` predicate that is true immediately asserts nothing.** The
  first version of the batch render leg waited on `outcome != "pending"`; the
  real default is `"waiting"`, so the wait fell straight through and the test
  read a table SCAN had not filled. It looked exactly like a failing fix. Fixed
  by waiting on the real settled outcome and adding a precondition assertion.
- **Two of the five tests were asserting against a configuration the app never
  reaches** — a single-file `_select_files` call, which the wizard routes to the
  single-file preview flow instead. Now a real two-file batch, which also lets
  the leg assert that a clean file's Errors cell stays blank.

## What was deferred

Nothing new. Three pre-existing items this change makes newly reachable or newly
visible were filed during the spec's review and stay out of scope, per § 9:

- **FIBR-0253** — the FIBR-0146 preview banner gives CSV-only advice on a PDF or
  OFX import. Re-raised by the correctness lane as a LOW; already tracked, and
  the spec discloses it in § 6.
- **FIBR-0254** — `report_line` contradicts the Errors column on an
  `already_imported` file, and mis-pluralises "1 rows couldn't be read".
- **FIBR-0255** — the silent under-import the spec's § 6 uncovered: a
  money-moving error row on a statement that prints no closing balance passes
  every gate, because on those layouts there is no gate to pass. This change
  ships the visibility half — it converts that case from *silent* to *reported*,
  which is the strongest single argument for it. Closing the gap itself is a
  parser change with its own design questions.
